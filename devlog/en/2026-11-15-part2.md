### 5. Walking as far as possible when blocked (`route_steps.py`)
- Places a TargetPoint `Route_NNN` (`Editor/Route`) every 175 cm along the PathNode chains, tagged `RouteStep` and `Prog:<goal>:<remaining cm>`. Remaining length is computed with Dijkstra over the node graph. Level 2 has 133 points.
- When `BP_PathfindingCharacter.StartRun` can't reach the goal, it walks to the reachable point with the smallest `Prog` (e.g. in front of an unrotated bridge), then reports BLOCKED.
- `rebuild()` regenerates these points automatically.
- **Pitfalls**:
  - Originally used "the PathNode closest to the goal": the character walked to the dead end at the top of a T junction because it was closest in a straight line.
  - With remaining length along the route, the character stops in front of the bridge.
