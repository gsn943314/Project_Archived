## Completed

### 1. Simplified Chinese in the main menu

- `BP_PuzzleGameInstance.Language`: 0 English, 1 Traditional Chinese, 2 Simplified Chinese; the language button cycles with `Percent_IntInt((Language+1), 3)`.
- `BP_MenuPlayerController.UpdateTexts` uses nested `SelectString` (Traditional / Simplified / English); the strings are generated as DSL by `/tmp/dsl/gen_menu_dsl.py` and written with `BlueprintDslToolset.Write`.
- PIE screenshots confirmed the Simplified Chinese font renders correctly.

### 2. `Lvl_BlockTutorial_V5_Art`: MegaJam art (`/Game/Dev/Meshes`)

- Copied from V5; `apply_art()` in `Content/Python/puzzle_art.py` (menu **Puzzle → Apply Art**) generates the art into the `Art` folder, without collision.
- Mesh mapping (settled over several rounds of Point and Yap feedback):
  - Stairs → `SM_PinkStone01` (175 cm per tier, mesh height squashed to about 0.5)
  - Walkway floor → `SM_StonePlatform01`
  - Walkway edges → continuous edges merged into one run and tiled at original size (scale 1): 3 cells `SM_StoneWall01`, 2 cells `SM_StoneWallDecor`, 1 cell `SM_StoneWallWindow`; walls interlock at corners
  - Both sides of stairs → sloped `SM_StoneWallDamaged` (flat cell + stair cell), position/rotation taken from the designer's hand-tuned `Art_Ramp_r5_c4` (constants `RAMP_YAW/ALONG/LAT_C/LAT`); skipped when the bottom of the stairs is a corner
  - Point B → `SM_Portal` facing the path, no wall on the entrance side
- `Editor_Grid` tag `Art`: walls on the camera side of stairs are no longer lowered (no notch).

**Pitfalls**:

- At one point tiers were 350 cm (tag `TierH:350`). With 25 cm steps on a 45° stair the NavMesh (19 cm voxels) saw a 50 cm drop and couldn't climb; 10 cm steps worked. Later switched back to 175 cm as requested.
- Wall orientation: I misjudged which way `SM_StoneWallDamaged` rises from screenshots; in the end I read the transform of the designer's hand-tuned piece and derived the formula from it.

### 3. Island edges (all Rebuild levels)

- `puzzle_level_tools.geometry_specs`: flood fill from the border; outer wall cells that don't touch a walkway aren't generated. Only walls touching the walkway remain, with thickness `RIM` = 175 cm (outer half cell trimmed).
- `Maze_Backdrop` lowered to `BACKDROP_Z` = -1800 so the level looks like a floating island.
- Island levels no longer get decorative outer walls (`edge()` in `puzzle_art`).
- Applied to `Lvl_Camp_Whitebox_Davidtesting` and `Lvl_BlockTutorial_V5_Art`.

### 4. Greybox / final view toggle

- Both sets are StaticMeshActors; only visibility changes. Collision and NavMesh always come from `Maze`.
- Tags: `VisFinal` (art in folders whose name contains `Art`, including hand-placed pieces), `VisGrey` (`GB_` proxies in the `Greybox` folder and `Maze` floor/stair blocks covered by art).
- `Content/Python/view_mode.py`:
  - `sync_greybox()` generates `GB_` greybox pieces from the art's current transforms
  - Shape table `/Game/Project/Data/DT_GreyboxProfiles` (struct `S_GreyboxProfile`: Mesh / Shape (Box/Steps/Custom/None) / Steps / StepAxis / CustomMesh / Padding); `SM_StoneWallDamaged` is 4 steps along `-X`
- Toolbar **Grey / Final** (uses temporarily-hidden in the Editor so the map isn't dirtied), **Apply Art**; menu **Reset Selected Art**.
- In game: `BP_PathfindingPlayerController.SetViewMode(bFinal)` (`GetAllActorsWithTag` + `SetActorHiddenInGame`); `EnterPlanning` shows greybox, `StartRun` shows the final view.

### 5. Protecting hand-edited art

- Generated art carries `VisFinal` + `ArtAuto` + `Gen:<transform>`.
- `prepare_art()`: pieces that were moved/rotated/scaled lose `ArtAuto` (locked); untouched ones are deleted and regenerated; generated slots that are now missing (deleted/replaced) are recorded as `Slot:` tags on `Art_Registry` and not refilled.
- `reset_selected_art()`: restores the selected pieces to their generated version.
- Tested: portal moved up 100 → locked after Apply Art; after Reset it returned to its original position with `ArtAuto` restored.

### 6. Edit-mode controls

- Left-drag on empty space = pan (`StartLeftPan`); wheel = zoom; right-drag left/right = orbit yaw around the screen center (fixed pitch).
- Camera rotation during PLAY removed.
- The "Shortcuts" text in the main menu updated in all three languages.

**Pitfalls**:

- Right-drag orbit first used `GetMousePosition`; while the right button is held the cursor is captured and doesn't move, so rotation was 0. Switched to `GetInputMouseDelta`.

### 7. Path node renumbering tool

- Menu **Puzzle → Renumber Path (selection order)**: Ctrl-click path nodes in order → renamed `PathA_00..`; unselected nodes become `UnusedNode_NN` (not deleted). Asks for confirmation first.
- Reason: nodes duplicated with Alt-drag get names like `PathA_3` that break the order.
- `Lvl_Camp_Whitebox_Davidtesting`: rebuilt from the designer's 13 ordered nodes; added `Hole_r5_c9` and `Block_Fill_Stairs` above the stairs. PIE: reaches B once the hole is filled, blocked otherwise.
