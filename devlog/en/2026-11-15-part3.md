### 3. Fox look progression (`Pathfinding/BP_FoxLook`, copied from the teammate's Dev version)
- **Level 0**:
  - Starts in full colour. When the opening cutscene ends and greybox begins (`bWaitIntro`: watches the controller's `bInIntro` go from true back to false), it turns into the grey model.
  - The grey model has procedural bobbing while walking: `WalkT` accumulates with speed, and sine waves drive bounce, sway and twist.
  - Reaching `MonumentStand` (tag `LookStand`) in front of the tower (`SM_StoneMonument`, yaw set to 0 so it faces the path), the fox faces the tower, plays the "asset repair" sequence and becomes the white model.
- **Level 1**: starts white; at `MonumentStand` (350, 525) beside the stone tablet (`SM_MossStone2`) next to the exit, it becomes full colour.
- **Level 2**: full colour from the start.
- Detour changes:
  - Added `AddSnapped` so corners align to cell centers.
  - No detour if the stand point is already on the route.
  - The detour is only inserted when this run really goes to the exit (`RunDestination` is the exit and at the same height).

### 4. Level 0 blocked hint
- TargetPoint `CliffStop` (tag `BlockedStop`, three `Say:` lines) at (-890, 350), right against the mountain.
- `BP_PathfindingCharacter.StartRun`: when the goal is unreachable and an `Editable` block is still within 1000 cm of the stop (the block hasn't been moved), the fox stops at this point.
- Controller:
  - `ShowHint`: turns to face the block and stores the lines.
  - After BLOCKED returns the fox to start, `StartHintDialog` / `UpdateHint` show each line in `WBP_Dialogue` for 2.8 s, then close.
  - Then a bobbing yellow arrow and outline are drawn on the block until it's moved, a pit is filled, or PLAY is pressed.
- While stopped at the mountain (`bHintPending`), `UpdateOcclusion` doesn't cut out.
- `IntroSpot` moved to (-1165, -18) so the fox stands directly in front of the notice board during the opening.
- Fixed the missing `PointA_PathfindingCharacter` in Level 0: re-placed it at the original position targeting `PointB_Target`. Couldn't find why it vanished.

### 5. Level 1 stops at the pit when blocked
- Ran `route_steps.build()`: 73 RouteSteps, and `PointB_Target` got its `Goal:` tag.

### 6. Grid-aligned walking (`BP_PathfindingCharacter.SquareHops`)
- If two path points are diagonal and at the same height, an L-shaped corner is inserted: X then Y, or Y first if that's blocked; both legs must pass `NavigationRaycast`.
- Called by `StartRun` and by FoxLook's detour.
- **Pitfalls**:
  - Originally only corners were snapped to cell centers. In open areas the NavMesh returns diagonals; Level 1's last leg cut diagonally from (350,525) to the exit (0,875), clipping the steps and the side of the arch.

### 7. Walking into the portal (`BP_PathfindingPlayerController.EnterPortal`)
- On reaching a goal that leads to a next level or ending:
  - The fox walks 3 m further forward (a separate `PortalHops` array sets `Hops`).
  - `NS_PortalMotes` plays in front.
  - After 0.5 s the camera fades to warm white over 1.2 s (`StartCameraFade`), then switches level or plays the ending.
