### 3. Audio system `BP_SoundDirector`

Spawned by `BP_PathfindingGameMode.BeginPlay`; watches game state every frame and plays sounds without touching other Blueprints (`d844e18`). Assets are placeholders synthesized by `Tools/Audio/gen_sfx.py`; importing assets with the same names replaces them later.

- Fox / enemy footsteps, knockdown, picking up / dropping blocks, lift / rotating bridge / one-way gate, full-screen and object Glitch, look-change shimmer, BGM + ambience, dialogue line changes (different for system / fox).
- v2 (`86b0d7f`): 4 soft Glitch sounds picked at random, hum + click for bridge / gate, new pick-up sound, portal sound; enemy footstep volume multiplied by distance to the fox (the listener is the camera, not the fox).
- In greybox, music and ambience are muffled: 650 Hz low-pass, volume ×0.7, smoothed over about 0.5 s (`9e16775`).
- Fox footsteps changed to pitchless filtered-noise taps; one step every 90 cm, volume 0.32 (`6da4bfa`).

**Pitfalls**:
- The first footsteps had pitch and sounded electronic; noise taps sounded natural.
- A 55 cm stride was too dense and heavy; set to 90 cm.
